Disclaimers & Limitations
  • Best Effort: Bootube is highly effective but cannot guarantee 100% accuracy. Some profanity may occasionally slip through.
  • Caption Dependent: Filtering relies entirely on Closed Captions (CC). If a video lacks captions (e.g. music videos, Shorts), the filter cannot operate.
  • Caption Quirks: We are at the mercy of the caption provider. Odd spellings, weird formatting, or overlapping dialogue can cause missed words.
  • Visual Content: Bootube only filters audio and subtitles. It does not censor inappropriate visual scenes or text burned into the video itself.
  • Live Streams: Auto-generated captions on live broadcasts are often delayed or inaccurate, reducing reliability.
  • Platform Differences: The extension's interface may render slightly differently depending on your operating system (Mac vs. Windows).
